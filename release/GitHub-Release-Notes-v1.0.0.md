It does **not** claim that UIERU has already demonstrated general performance superiority, autonomous recursive self-improvement, or consciousness.

## 1. Release Overview

UIERU proposes a reusable organizational unit for coordinating human, AI, LLM, ANN, agent, service, and tool capabilities through explicit contracts, executable calling paths, forward-guiding scoring structures, evidence-preserving relay, and governed structural evolution.

Its canonical abstraction is:

**UIERU = (K, P, F, R, E)**

| Component | Meaning | Primary responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| **K — Contract** | Subject, goals, authority, constraints, evidence and acceptance criteria | Define what the unit may do and what counts as success |
| **P — Calling Path** | An executable route through a Calling Graph | Coordinate action and delegation |
| **F — Scoring Forest** | Multi-perspective evaluation and control | Guide selection before and during execution; assess outcomes afterward |
| **R — Relay** | Portable, provenance-aware structural handoff | Preserve context, evidence, failures and receiver autonomy |
| **E — Evolution** | Governed structural modification | Test, accept, version or roll back candidate improvements |

UIERU is intended as **selective structural containerization**: use it where coordination, evidence, portability or adaptive organization justify the overhead, not as a compulsory wrapper around every operation.

## 2. Key Research Contributions

### 2.1 Calling Path and Scoring Forest duality

The Calling Path describes **what is executed**; the Scoring Forest evaluates **which routes, outcomes and changes are preferable** under a specified subject, contract and perspective. The two are connected but not interchangeable.

### 2.2 Forward guidance and multi-trail intelligence

Evaluation is moved from a purely retrospective step toward guidance **before, during and after** execution. Candidate trails can be separated into **Exploit**, **Explore** and **Challenge**, with explicit budgets and decision gates.

### 2.3 Sandbox threading and counter-evidence

Alternative approaches are isolated and compared under controlled conditions. Negative findings, failure traces and counter-evidence are retained instead of discarded when a preferred candidate succeeds.

### 2.4 Composable and nested intelligence units

UIERUs may be assembled sequentially, in parallel, hierarchically or in federated arrangements, provided contracts, scoring perspectives, authority boundaries and failure handling are reconciled.

### 2.5 Structural relay and bidirectional collective learning

A relay portfolio may carry contracts, structural representations, provenance, evaluation, residuals, limitations and counter-evidence. Receiving subjects validate and localize transferred intelligence rather than automatically inheriting the sender's authority or judgments. Feedback may flow in both directions.

### 2.6 Residual-driven structural evolution

Candidate changes arise from residuals, failures, unmet objectives and new evidence. Independent evaluation, holdout tasks, version control, rollback and governance distinguish **structural change** from **verified improvement**.

### 2.7 Three research extensions

- **Subject-indexed world models and structural memory:** subject-specific perception, episodic memory, world models, self-models and evaluation sovereignty.
- **Big-animal intelligence organization:** platform-scale distributed scoring, capability registries, governance and structural recursive improvement.
- **Subject sovereignty and collective intelligence ecosystems:** interoperable intelligence exchange without automatic transfer of authority or control.

These extensions define research directions, not demonstrated implementations.

## 4. Validation and Reproducibility

The release includes a proposed validation program rather than a completed benchmark suite. It separates:

1. **Conformance:** whether implementations respect contracts, provenance, sandbox isolation, authority boundaries and rollback rules.
2. **Comparative effectiveness:** whether UIERU improves outcomes against cost-matched workflow, agent-orchestration, single-path and post-hoc-evaluation baselines.
3. **Generalization:** whether gains persist on unseen tasks and under changed conditions.
4. **Governance:** whether evaluation remains independent and receivers retain control over adoption.

See [`TESTABLE-HYPOTHESES.md`](../TESTABLE-HYPOTHESES.md) for H01–H16 and [`VALIDATION-ROADMAP.md`](../VALIDATION-ROADMAP.md) for phased experiments and Go / Revise / No-Go gates.

**Key distinctions:**

- Conformance is not proof of performance improvement.
- Structural change is not proof of intelligence improvement.
- Portability of evidence is not transfer of decision authority.
- A universal architectural vocabulary is not proof of universal advantage.

## 5. Intended Audience

Researchers and practitioners working on structural intelligence, AI agent orchestration, evaluation and control, distributed intelligence, human–AI collaboration, knowledge transfer, AI governance, and recursive improvement.

## 6. Known Limitations

- The proposed architecture requires empirical comparison and implementation-level testing.
- Multi-trail exploration, evidence retention and governance introduce nontrivial compute and coordination costs.
- Contract reconciliation and multi-perspective scoring may be difficult in open-ended or adversarial settings.
- Receiver-side localization can fail when assumptions, authority or evidence are incompatible.
- Subject models are functional engineering abstractions; they do not establish phenomenal consciousness.
- Platform-scale structural RSI remains an open research problem, not a proven deployment capability.
