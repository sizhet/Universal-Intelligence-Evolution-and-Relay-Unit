Universal Intelligence Evolution and Relay Unit (UIERU) is an engineering-first research framework for organizing, coordinating, relaying, and evolving composable intelligence across humans, AI agents, models, services, and institutions. Its canonical unit integrates a governed contract, an executable Calling Path, a forward-guiding Scoring Forest, evidence-preserving Structural Relay, and validated structural evolution. The research explores multi-trail sandboxing and counter-evidence, nested and federated composition, bidirectional collective learning, and structurally governed recursive self-improvement. Three extensions investigate subject-indexed world and self models, large-scale AI platform intelligence organization, and subject sovereignty in collective intelligence ecosystems. The repository provides ten core research papers, three extensions, three cases, four engineering specifications, conceptual figures, and an open validation agenda with testable hypotheses. UIERU is a proposed architecture, not a claim of demonstrated universal performance improvement; its benefits require independent comparative evaluation. Authors: Sizhe Tan and GPT-Obot.
