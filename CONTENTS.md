# CONTENTS — Universal Intelligence Evolution and Relay Unit (UIERU)

**From Governed Calling Paths and Scoring Forests to Multi-Trail Exploration, Collective Relay, and Open-Ended Structural Evolution**

**Authors:** Sizhe Tan & GPT-Obot  
**Version:** v1.0.0  
**Approach:** **Engineering First · Theory Supported · Research Open**  
**Status:** Research architecture, proposed engineering specifications, illustrative cases, and testable hypotheses

> **No prerequisite DOI required.** UIERU is a self-contained engineering-first research repository. References to other Structural Intelligence projects provide optional background, not required reading.

## 1. Repository at a Glance

**UIERU = Execution Path + Forward Guidance + Relay + Evolution.**

A Universal Intelligence Evolution and Relay Unit is a composable organizational structure for coordinating existing human and machine capabilities. It connects a governed Calling Path with a forward-guiding Scoring Forest, preserves execution evidence and alternatives, supports structural relay, and enables independently validated structural improvement. UIERU does not replace LLMs, agents, tools, workflows, or human decision-makers.

The canonical research model is:

```text
U = (K, P, F, R, E)

K — Contract: subject, purpose, authority, scope, constraints
P — Calling Path: decomposition, orchestration, execution
F — Scoring Forest: forward guidance, control, evaluation
R — Relay: evidence, provenance, portfolio, handoff
E — Evolution: residuals, multi-trail learning, validated structural delta
```

**Document inventory:** 10 core papers + 3 cases + 4 engineering specifications + 3 research extensions = **20 substantive documents**. The repository also includes navigation, research validation, figure references, and release metadata.

## 11. Interpretation and Research Boundaries

- **Organization, not replacement:** UIERU coordinates existing intelligence; it does not claim to supersede all agent or workflow architectures.
- **Selective adoption:** Small tasks may not benefit from a full UIERU runtime. Coordination, evidence, and governance overhead must be measured.
- **Guidance is not authority:** A Scoring Forest may rank or recommend actions but cannot grant permissions absent from the governing contract.
- **Portability is not automatic trust:** Relayed structures require recipient-side verification, localization, and appropriate authorization.
- **Structural RSI is governed:** Proposed improvements must be independently tested and authorized, with versioning and rollback.
- **Subject indexing is not consciousness:** Subject-based world models and memory indexing are functional architectural proposals, not claims about subjective experience.
- **Universality is a hypothesis:** Generality, net value, and interoperability require comparative evidence and explicit failure criteria.
